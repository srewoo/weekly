Privacy

Notes PIN

Private notes are encrypted on this computer with your PIN. A forgotten PIN cannot be recovered.

How private notes are protected

Weekly never stores the PIN. Five wrong tries pause unlocking for 30 seconds, then longer. Removing the PIN turns private notes back into ordinary ones. If you export your data, use 6 to 8 digits: an export holds the PIN check value, and a 4-digit PIN can be guessed from a copy of the file.

Integrations

Adds a Post to Slack button to your update. “Copy as → Slack” works without it. The webhook stays on this computer and is never exported.

Your data

Weekly keeps your data only in this Chrome profile. There is no sync, so an export is your backup.

Your PIN is set, so an export also holds what checks it: use a PIN of 6 to 8 digits.

An export holds your tasks, notes, settings and activity. Private notes stay encrypted, but the file is enough to try PINs against. Keep export files private.

Import takes a Weekly export from this or another computer, a Daybook backup or a ForgetMeNot export.
