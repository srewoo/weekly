Weekly

Your week, written up for you.

Weekly turns the tickets, merge requests and docs you worked on into a ready-to-send weekly update or standup, grouped for you. You read it, untick anything private, and copy it. It also keeps your tasks and notes.

What tracking records

  • The address and title of the tab you’re looking at, and for how long
  • Only while Chrome is in front and you’re at the keyboard

What it never does

  • Read what’s on a page, your keystrokes, or take screenshots
  • Send anything anywhere. It stays on this computer until you copy or post it

Track my week?

Weekly notes which tab is in front, so your update starts itself. Chrome will call this “read your browsing history”. Say no and nothing is recorded.

Want a head start? Weekly can rebuild your last 15 days from Chrome’s history, so your first update isn’t empty. Times are estimates, marked with ~. Chrome will ask you to allow this.
